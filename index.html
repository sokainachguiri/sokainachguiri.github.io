02

Making second-hand fashion feel like a find

RetroChic Maroc Social Media & Digital Content Manager Jan–Apr 2024
The brief
RetroChic is a Moroccan app where people buy and sell pre-loved fashion. The brand needed content that made second-hand feel desirable, and that got people both shopping and listing their own clothes.
What I did
Created French-language Instagram content for @retrochicmaroc: trend-led editorial posts like the capri pant and denim edits, animated product roundups such as “Tops basiques”, community posts, and a POV reel, “the life of a forgotten dress”, showing how quickly you can list an item and sell it on the app. I also ran influencer collaborations end to end with micro- and macro-influencers, and launched and optimized paid campaigns in Meta Ads.
Highlight
A post asking followers what was in their RetroChic basket drew a run of replies from shoppers sharing their finds, from a new-with-tags Zuhair Murad bag to a Dolce & Gabbana swimsuit.
